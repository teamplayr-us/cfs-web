How an Event Works

Showcase Combine & Camp

Each athlete’s chance to stand out individually — position-specific skill work and 1v1s in front of college coaches, plus verified combine testing measured by trained staff and on the record. Any athlete can register; results feed the athlete recruiting package coaches receive.
