Secret 1 of 5

01

Your transcript
recruits too.

Coaches check grades before they fall in love with your game. Some schools admit fewer than half of their applicants — and coaches have had to walk away from recruits who couldn’t get in academically.

Your GPA is part of your film.

Secret 2 of 5

02

Character gets
the offer.

Before athleticism, coaches ask one question: can she thrive here — on the team, on campus, in the community? Selfless athletes make better teams, and coaches recruit accordingly.

Who you are travels with how you play.

collegeflagshowcase.com

Secret 3 of 5

03

Verified beats
self-reported.

A 40 time from your backyard doesn’t move a coach. Numbers measured by trained staff, on the record, are numbers a coach can trust — and act on.

If it’s not verified, it’s just a claim.

Secret 4 of 5

04

Get evaluated
in person.

Film shows your highlights. In person, coaches see technique, effort between reps, and how you compete when it matters. The athletes who get recruited put themselves where coaches are actually watching.

Highlights get attention. Live reps get offers.

Secret 5 of 5

05

Every level is
an opportunity.

Division I is just arriving in flag — but DII, DIII, NAIA, and NJCAA programs are recruiting right now, with scholarship money at many. Athletes who only chase one level miss the coaches ready to say yes.

Know the levels. Get in front of all of them.
