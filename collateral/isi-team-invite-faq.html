Team Invitation FAQ.

Answers for invited team coaches and club directors. Anything not covered here — just reply to your invitation email, or write us at info@5v5sports.com (888.350.2215 ext. 2).

Accepting & Registering

How do we accept our invitation?

Complete your team registration through Zorts using the link in your invitation email — entry fee, rosters, and age verification all run through the International Superflag Invitational.

How do we secure our spot?

A $50 per-team deposit within 10 days of your invitation confirms your acceptance and secures your spot. The deposit applies toward your $550 team entry, and the balance is due by November 22, 2026.

What is the refund policy?

Refund requests must be made at least 21 days before the event; requests inside that window are reviewed case by case. Events canceled due to circumstances beyond our control receive credit toward future events.

How is age eligibility determined?

By each athlete’s age as of August 1, verified through Zorts during registration.

The Tournament

What divisions compete?

Boys 8U, 10U, 12U, 14U, 16U, and 18U, and girls 8U, 10U, 12U, 14U, 16U, and 18U, with a strict team cap per division.

What rules and format does the tournament use?

Games are 5v5 under NFL Flag Hybrid rules, sanctioned by iFlag — three games of round-robin play, then single-elimination playoffs.

Who competes?

The field is invitation-only, curated by the Selection Committee from top travel programs across the United States and international teams from Mexico, Panama, and beyond.

Schedule & Travel

When should we arrive?

Plan to arrive Friday, December 11 — tournament games can begin as early as 8:00 AM Saturday.

Which airports should traveling teams use?

Dallas/Fort Worth International (DFW) and Dallas Love Field (DAL), each about 35 miles from the complex.

Where is the venue?

Craig Ranch Sports Complex, 6375 Collin McKinney Parkway, McKinney, TX.

Where do we stay?

This is a Stay & Play tournament — all commuting teams must book through the tournament housing service as a condition of acceptance. Please don’t book directly with hotels. Housing questions: 505-346-0522.

Girls 12U–18U — The College Flag Showcase

What is the College Flag Showcase?

Girls teams 12U–18U also participate in the College Flag Showcase — the college recruiting layer of the weekend. College flag football coaches evaluate from the sideline all weekend, and athletes can register individually for the Showcase Combine & Camp on Friday afternoon. Details at collegeflagshowcase.com.
