Team Invitation FAQ.

Answers for invited team coaches and club directors. Anything not covered here — just reply to your invitation email or write us at info@collegeflagshowcase.com.

Accepting & Registering

How do we accept our invitation?

Complete your team registration through Zorts using the private link in your invitation email. Entry fee, rosters, and age verification all run through the International Superflag Invitational. Please don’t share the link outside your organization.

How do we secure our spot?

A $50 per-team deposit within 10 days of your invitation confirms your acceptance and secures your spot. The deposit applies toward your $550 team entry, and the balance is due by November 22, 2026.

What does the $550 team entry cover?

Your team’s entry in the Travel Team Showcase Tournament on Saturday and Sunday, December 12–13.

How is age eligibility determined?

By each athlete’s age as of August 1, verified through Zorts during registration.

The Tournament

What are the divisions?

Girls 12U, 14U, 16U, and 18U. Each division is capped at 24 teams.

What rules does the tournament use?

NFL Flag Hybrid rules, sanctioned by iFlag. Games are 5v5.

What is the schedule?

The Showcase Combine & Camp runs Friday, December 11, from 3:00 to 5:30 PM. Tournament games begin at 8:00 AM on Saturday. Detailed game schedules are confirmed closer to the event.

What is the International Superflag Invitational?

The tournament is held in partnership with the International Superflag Invitational — an invitation-only international championship that drew over 60 elite teams from across the world in 2025. Your registration runs through the ISI.

Are games filmed?

Every game is filmed in the full-field view college coaches use to evaluate.

The Recruiting Weekend

Who is evaluating?

College flag football coaches from NCAA DI, DII, DIII, NAIA, and NJCAA programs evaluate from the sideline all weekend — and the board is growing at collegeflagshowcase.com/colleges.

Is the Showcase Combine & Camp included in team entry?

No — each athlete registers individually at collegeflagshowcase.com ($175 standard). Athletes on your tournament roster receive a $50 discount code by separate email, bringing it to $125.

What do our athletes get out of the weekend?

Verified combine testing and live skill work in front of college coaches on Friday, real competition with those coaches on the sideline all weekend, and inclusion in the recruiting package college coaches receive.

Logistics

Where is the venue?

Craig Ranch Sports Complex, 6375 Collin McKinney Parkway, McKinney, TX.

Where do we stay?

Dallas is a Stay & Play event — booking through the tournament housing service is a condition of acceptance. Partner hotels include AC Hotel McKinney, Fairfield Inn & Suites Dallas McKinney, Hilton Dallas Plano Granite Park, and Marriott Dallas Allen. Housing details come with your acceptance.

Which airports should traveling teams use?

Dallas/Fort Worth International (DFW) and Dallas Love Field (DAL).

Is there anything for coaches on Friday night?

Invited team coaches are welcome at the Coaches Welcome Event on Friday night. Details come with your credentials.
