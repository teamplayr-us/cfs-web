Why This Matters

College flag football is real and growing fast. Flag debuts at the 2028 Olympics, it’s an NCAA Emerging Sport for Women with scholarships live today, and 90+ college programs are playing with more added every season. College coaches attend our events to recruit — this weekend is how your athlete gets in front of them.

How the Weekend Works

01

Individual Showcase Combine & Camp

Friday · open registration

Her chance to stand out individually — position-specific skill work and 1v1s in front of college coaches, plus verified combine testing measured by trained staff and on the record. No team required; any athlete can register.

02

Travel Team Showcase Tournament

Sat–Sun · invited teams only

5v5 competition between invited travel programs, with college coaches evaluating from the sideline. Athletes on competing teams get $50 off combine registration.
