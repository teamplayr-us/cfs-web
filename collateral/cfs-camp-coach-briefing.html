Coaching Brief.

Prepared for Coach Saaid Mortazavi — what the camp is, who’s on the field, and how the weekend runs.

What the Camp Is

The Showcase Combine & Camp opens each College Flag Showcase Series event weekend. It’s individual and open-registration — girls flag football athletes compete on their own, not with a team — and everything happens in front of credentialed college coaches. Every athlete’s combine numbers and camp participation feed the athlete recruiting package those coaches receive.

The Athletes

  • Girls 12U · 14U · 16U · 18U (age as of Aug 1)
  • Individual, open registration — no team required
  • Serious travel-program athletes; many rosters attend around the invite-only tournament
  • Every participant is in the recruiting package college coaches receive

Combine Testing

Measured by trained staff, on the record:

  • Height, weight & wingspan
  • 20-yard dash (10-yard split)
  • 5-10-5 pro agility
  • Standing broad jump · vertical jump
  • Position-specific skills evaluation

The Camp Session

  • Position-specific skill work run live in front of college coaches
  • 1v1s — each athlete’s chance to show her game individually
  • Coaching-led structure — format and stations shaped with you

Who’s Watching

  • Credentialed college coaches on-site all weekend
  • Committed today: NCAA DII, DIII, NAIA, and NJCAA programs — nine colleges on the board and growing
  • Tournament games filmed by AI cameras in the full-field evaluation view
