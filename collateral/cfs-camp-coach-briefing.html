The Event Weekend
Brief.

The full Dallas event weekend — the Showcase Combine & Camp, the Showcase Tournament, and the college coaches evaluating throughout.

The Weekend at a Glance

The College Flag Showcase Series is a girls national recruiting series — ten events across the 2026–27 season, evaluated in person by college flag football coaches. Each weekend pairs a verified combine and coached camp with an invite-only travel-team tournament. Event 01 runs December 11–13 at Craig Ranch Sports Complex in McKinney, in partnership with the International Superflag Invitational.

01 · Combine & Camp — Your Session

  • Friday, 3:00–5:30 PM — individual, open registration; girls 12U–18U (age as of Aug 1)
  • Verified combine: height/weight/wingspan, 20-yard dash (10-yard split), 5-10-5, broad & vertical jumps — trained staff, on the record
  • Coached camp: position-specific skill work and 1v1s, run live in front of college coaches — format shaped with you

02 · Showcase Tournament

  • Sat–Sun — invite-only 5v5 between top travel programs, domestic and international
  • Held in partnership with the International Superflag Invitational — 60+ elite teams from across the world in 2025
  • Every game filmed by AI cameras in the full-field view college coaches evaluate with

03 · The College Coaches

  • Credentialed college coaches evaluate all weekend — camp Friday, sideline Sat–Sun
  • Committed today: NCAA DII, DIII, NAIA, and NJCAA programs — nine colleges on the board and growing
  • Coaches Welcome Event Friday night for college and invited team coaches

The Athletes

  • Serious travel-program athletes from across the country and internationally
  • Camp is each athlete’s individual stage; the tournament shows her in live competition
  • Every camp participant is in the athlete recruiting package college coaches receive
