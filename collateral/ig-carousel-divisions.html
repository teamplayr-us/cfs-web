The Levels · 1 of 6

NCAA Division I

Who plays here

The top recruits in each sport, at the largest athletic programs in the country.

The commitment

The highest — training, film, travel, and season run most of the year.

Scholarships

Athletic scholarships available.

Flag football today

Just arriving. The first Division I conference added women’s flag in 2024; Nebraska becomes the first Power Four school with varsity flag in 2028.

The Levels · 2 of 6

NCAA Division II

Who plays here

Strong athletes who want high-level varsity competition with more room for the rest of college life.

The commitment

Serious in-season demands, with more balance across the year than Division I.

Scholarships

Athletic scholarships available — most are partial awards.

Flag football today

Active and recruiting — DII coaches are committed to evaluate at the series today.

collegeflagshowcase.com

The Levels · 3 of 6

NCAA Division III

Who plays here

Strong high school athletes who want real varsity competition alongside a full academic life. The NCAA’s largest division by number of schools.

The commitment

A structured season and practice schedule, with the most academic flexibility in the NCAA.

Scholarships

No athletic scholarships — DIII schools award academic and need-based aid instead.

Flag football today

One of the most active levels in the sport — several DIII programs are on the series board now.

collegeflagshowcase.com

The Levels · 4 of 6

NAIA

Who plays here

Athletes at smaller four-year colleges with competitive varsity athletics.

The commitment

Full varsity seasons with regular practice and travel schedules.

Scholarships

Athletic scholarships available.

Flag football today

The most established level — the NAIA sponsored women’s flag first, with official seasons since 2021.

collegeflagshowcase.com

The Levels · 5 of 6

NJCAA Junior College

Who plays here

Athletes starting at two-year colleges — play immediately, develop, then transfer to a four-year program.

The commitment

Full varsity seasons in a two-year window.

Scholarships

Available at many NJCAA schools.

Flag football today

Active and growing — NJCAA flag programs are recruiting now.

collegeflagshowcase.com

The Levels · 6 of 6

Club

Who plays here

Students at schools without varsity flag — anyone who wants to keep competing.

The commitment

Set by the team — practices and tournaments without the varsity schedule.

Scholarships

None — club sports don’t carry athletic scholarships.

Flag football today

A way to keep playing at schools that haven’t added varsity flag yet, as more programs launch every season.
