What is the College Flag Showcase?

The College Flag Showcase Series is a national recruiting series for girls flag football — ten events across the 2026–27 season, each evaluated in person by college flag football coaches. Dallas is the first. The Showcase Combine & Camp is the individual day: you test, compete in position work and 1v1s, and get seen by college coaches — no team needed.
