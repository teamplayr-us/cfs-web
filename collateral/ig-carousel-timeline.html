Middle School

7–8

Grade

Fall in love
with the work.

Fundamentals, flag IQ, and playing everything you can. College coaches aren’t watching yet — this is where the athlete they’ll eventually want gets built.

No pressure yet. Just reps.

Freshman Year

09

Grade

Your clock
starts.

Your GPA counts from your first semester, and it follows you all the way to admissions. Start saving game film now, even if it’s just clips on your phone.

Freshman grades are recruiting decisions.

Sophomore Year

10

Grade

Get on
the record.

Real game film, verified combine numbers, and a recruiting profile that holds them. This is the year you go from playing the game to being findable in it.

Coaches can’t recruit what they can’t find.

collegeflagshowcase.com

Junior Year

11

Grade

Get in
the room.

Build a target list across every level, refresh your film, and put yourself in front of coaches — camps, showcases, real competition. Reach out to programs; don’t wait to be discovered.

This is the year coaches build their boards.

collegeflagshowcase.com

Senior Year

12

Grade

Finish
the drive.

Keep your film current and your grades up, answer every coach who’s recruiting you, and stay on top of applications and admissions. Opportunities stay open later than you think — DI is new, and programs at every level are still adding athletes.

Recruiting isn’t over until you commit.
