# College Flag Showcase Series — Brand Guidelines

Source of truth: collegeflagshowcase.com. Use these rules to make any
document, deck, graphic, or email read as the same brand as the website.

## Identity

- **Name:** College Flag Showcase Series (logo mark reads "Collegiate Flag
  Showcase Series"). Never abbreviate to "CFS" in public-facing material.
- **Tagline / hero:** "Ball Out. Get Seen. Get Recruited."
- **Positioning line:** Girls National Recruiting Series — a verified combine
  plus a college showcase tournament in one weekend, evaluated in person by
  college flag football coaches.
- **Attribution:** the series is independent — never call it "a 5v5 Sports
  production." Co-founded by Allen Hamilton (founder of a youth sports
  technology platform; lifelong athlete and coach), Monty Holloway, and
  Amanda Newman (both founders of 5v5 Sports). 5v5 Sports operates the
  International Superflag Invitational, which the first stop layers on
  top of — credit 5v5 Sports only in that role.
- **Logo:** circular badge, pink on black, sits on dark backgrounds. Square
  PNG (192×192). Don't recolor it or place it on busy imagery.

## Color

| Token | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Brand black ("navy") | `#0A0A0B` | Ink, dark section backgrounds, borders |
| Brand pink ("red") | `#FF2D8E` | THE action/accent color: buttons, highlights, key words, numbers |
| Pink hover | `#E0187A` | Hover/pressed state of brand pink |
| Chalk | `#F7F5F6` | Light background ("field white") — default page background |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Cards on chalk |
| Light pink ("gold") | `#FF8DC1` | Accents ONLY on dark backgrounds: verified ✓ marks, badges, hover states |
| Steel | `#5C5A5E` | Secondary text on light |
| Hairline (light) | `#E6E2E5` | Thin rules/borders on light |
| Hairline (dark) | `#242127` | Thin rules/field lines on black |
| Muted on dark | `#C9C4C9` | Body text on dark sections |

Rules: sections alternate black and chalk. Pink is used sparingly — one
accent word in a headline, buttons, numbers, key data marks — never as a
background wash (exception: a full-bleed pink call-to-action section).
Light pink (#FF8DC1) never appears on light backgrounds.

## Typography

- **Display: "Big Shoulders Display"** (weights 600/700/800, mostly 800).
  All headlines and buttons. ALWAYS UPPERCASE, tight line-height (~0.95).
  Big and condensed is the signature look.
- **Body: "Archivo"** (400/500/600/700). Sentence case, comfortable
  line-height (~1.55). Bold lead-in phrases inside paragraphs are common.
- **Data/labels: "JetBrains Mono"** (500/700). Small (10–13px), UPPERCASE,
  wide letter-spacing (0.12–0.22em). Used for: eyebrows/kickers, numbers
  ("01"), dates ("DEC 2026"), stat labels, tags, form labels. This mono
  layer is what makes materials look like the brand — use it for all
  metadata and labels.
- Google Fonts: all three are free on Google Fonts.

## Graphic motifs

- **Hard edges everywhere.** No rounded corners, no gradients, no soft
  drop shadows. Cards get 3px solid black borders.
- **Offset block shadows:** solid, un-blurred `8px 8px 0` in pink (featured
  cards) or black (standard cards).
- **Chalk yard-line dividers:** thin black rule with a mono label like
  "THE SEASON — 30 YD" and a dashed ticker — section dividers reference
  football field markings. Dark hero sections carry faint horizontal field
  lines.
- **Numbered lists** use two-digit mono numbers in pink: 01, 02, 03…
- **Verified checkmarks:** ✓ in small solid squares (pink on light, or
  light-pink on black); metrics can carry a superscript ✓ (e.g. 5.12✓) to
  mean "verified."
- **Buttons:** rectangular, uppercase display font, either solid pink with
  white text or transparent with a 3px border ("ghost").

## Voice

- Short, punchy, imperative. Headlines are commands or claims:
  "Ball Out." "One Weekend. Two Ways to Get Seen." "Put Your Brand
  on the Field."
- Em dashes join a bold claim to its explanation: "**Individual skill
  display and live tournament competition in one weekend** — evaluated in
  person by college flag football coaches…"
- Key vocabulary: **verified** (the brand's core promise), **events** (series
  language: "Events," "Event 01," "Next Event" — plain, never "stops," and
  the brand is a **series**, never "a tour" (generic references say "the
  series") in
  customer-facing copy), **Showcase Combine &
  Camp** (individual flow, open registration), **Showcase Tournament**
  (team flow, INVITE-ONLY — teams "request an invite," never "sign up"),
  **Girls 12U+**, **2026–27 Season**.
- Athlete-first, confident, zero hype-fluff. No exclamation marks. Speak to
  athletes directly ("your measurables," "bring your game") and to parents
  plainly.
