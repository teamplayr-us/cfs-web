# ISI-DALLAS.md — International Superflag Invitational partnership (Event 01)

Source of truth for how the Dallas event (Event 01, Dec 11–13, 2026) relates
to the **International Superflag Invitational (ISI)**. Vetted from the
official ISI Travel Team Information Packet (v1.0, from Monty, Aug 2026)
against the series' own facts. Where this doc and the packet disagree, the
resolutions below are the answer; where a fact is missing from both, ask the
founders.

Last updated: Aug 26, 2026.

## The relationship (approved framing)

- The Dallas **Showcase Tournament is held in partnership with the
  International Superflag Invitational** — an invitation-only international
  tournament operated by 5v5 Sports that drew over 60 elite teams from
  across the world in 2025.
- **Invited teams register for the ISI.** Team entry, payment, rosters, and
  age verification all run through the ISI's process (Zorts
  Sports), not through collegeflagshowcase.com. Our site is where teams
  *request an invite*; accepted teams get ISI registration instructions.
- The College Flag Showcase Series layers the college showcase on top:
  the Friday combine, credentialed college coaches all weekend, and the
  athlete recruiting package. The packet's page 8 documents this under
  "Girls Flag Combine & College Showcase Tournament."
- Attribution rule still applies: the series is independent — never
  "a 5v5 Sports production." 5v5 Sports is credited only as the ISI's
  operator; "in partnership with the International Superflag Invitational"
  is the approved phrase.
- **Shorthand:** full name on first mention, then **"the ISI"** — never
  "the Invitational" (founder rule, Aug 2026).

## Conflicts found and resolutions

1. **"Collegiate Flag Showcase Series" (packet p.8, twice).** Wrong — the
   brand is **College Flag Showcase Series**. Send back to Monty for v1.1.
2. **"See page 9 for college showcase details" (packet p.2).** The packet
   ends at page 8, where those details actually live. Fix in v1.1.
3. **Registration deadline.** The packet lists none; ours is **Nov 22,
   2026**. Ours stands on every series surface. Confirm with Monty that ISI
   registration honors the same date so teams hear one deadline.
4. **Divisions.** The ISI fields girls and boys 8U–18U; the **showcase
   covers girls 12U, 14U, 16U, 18U only** (combine and college evaluation).
   Series surfaces keep stating 12U–18U girls and never enumerate the ISI's
   full field; support answers may explain the distinction if a team asks
   about other divisions.
5. **Combine test list.** The packet itemizes combine tests (height/weight/
   wingspan, 20-yard dash with 10-yard split, 5-10-5 pro agility, broad
   jump, vertical, position-specific evaluation). Series surfaces have
   deliberately said "verified combine testing" without itemizing. The
   packet is team-facing and already in circulation, so the list is
   effectively public — but do not add it to series surfaces without the
   founder's sign-off.
6. **Invitation graphic naming.** Our official invitation card says teams
   are invited to "the College Flag Showcase"; teams then register for the
   ISI. Founder to decide whether the card should name the ISI
   (open item — do not change the template until decided).
