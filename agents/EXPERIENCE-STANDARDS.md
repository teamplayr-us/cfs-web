# CFSS-Wide Experience Standards

Founder-authored, Aug 2026. These are the principles every customer journey
in the College Flag Showcase Series must follow — before any
segment-specific design (athletes, parents, team coaches, college coaches,
sponsors). Internal document: "CFSS" is acceptable shorthand here, never in
anything public-facing.

Every experience decision — a page, an email, a sign, a check-in flow, a
staff script — should be checkable against these eight standards.

## 1. No Surprises

People should never have to hunt for basic information.

Schedules, addresses, parking, check-in, rules, timing, contacts and
expectations should arrive **before they need them**.

## 2. One Source of Truth

We cannot have:

- website saying one thing
- email saying another
- coach saying another
- staff member saying something else

Every experience flow needs a canonical operational source.

## 3. Segment-Specific Communication

A college coach should not receive the same communication as a parent.

The information architecture should essentially say:

> Who are you? Here is exactly what matters to you.

## 4. Mobile First

At an event, nobody wants to dig through a 14-page PDF.

The most frequently needed information should be accessible **in seconds
from a phone**.

## 5. Proactive > Reactive

World-class events eliminate questions rather than becoming great at
answering questions.

**Every repeated question becomes a design defect to fix for the next
event.**

## 6. Recognition Matters

People should feel like CFSS knows who they are.

Especially:

- college coaches
- participating team coaches
- athletes
- sponsors/partners

Credentials, names, personal welcomes and targeted communication all
matter.

## 7. The Event Should Feel Bigger Than It Is

This is particularly important for the athlete experience.

Even if this is an early CFSS event, the experience can feel national
through:

- presentation
- branding
- organization
- photography/video
- credentials
- announcements
- data
- recruiting presence
- staff professionalism

## 8. Finish Strong

The experience doesn't end when the last game ends.

For many segments, **post-event delivery is part of the product they
purchased**.

---

*For agents working in this repo: these standards govern experience and
communication design — event pages, emails, support answers, check-in
flows, printed materials. Standard 2 is why facts live in `data/*.ts` and
`agents/SUPPORT.md` and get updated everywhere in one pass. Standard 5
means recurring support questions should be reported back to the founder
as candidates for design fixes, not just answered.*
